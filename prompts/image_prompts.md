# General Guidelines for Image Generation

**Style**: Modern, clean, professional technical illustration
**Color Palette**: Use bright, optimistic colors with good contrast
**Aspect Ratio**: All images should be 16:9 (1920x1080 recommended)
**Consistency**: Maintain similar illustration style across all images
**Clarity**: Focus on clear visual communication over photorealism
**Text**: Avoid text in images (will be added as slide content)
**Technical Accuracy**: When showing 3D concepts, maintain accurate perspective and geometry

# Alternative Prompts for More Abstract Slides

If you prefer more abstract/minimal designs:

**Learning Journey Alternative**: Abstract geometric shapes forming an ascending staircase, each step representing a milestone, gradient from dark blue to bright yellow, minimal geometric art style

**Automation Alternative**: Circular flow diagram with icons (code → robot → files → images), all in continuous loop, flat design style, purple and blue gradient background

**Key Takeaways Alternative**: Three interconnected hexagons with icons inside (brain, code, 3D cube), connected by lines forming a triangle, modern infographic style, teal and white colors
